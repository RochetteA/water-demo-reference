For the demo: the machine can only make 2.5 and 11.5 with Electrolysis Enhancer loaded. Check you have some before Jenna arrives.

Drinking it

  • How much: half your body weight in ounces, every day.
  • Ramp up: 8.5 for the first week, 9.0 for the second week, then stay on 9.5. Only move up once any adjustment symptoms have settled (headache, change in bowel movements, dry mouth, nausea, skin rash).
  • Freshness: the hydrogen is a gas and is gone after about 48 hours. Fresh daily is best, every other day at the outside.
  • Medication: never take meds with 8.5 to 9.5 water. Use 7.0 or plain filtered water, and leave a gap before going back to Kangen water.
  • Morning: 2 to 3 cups first thing on an empty stomach.
  • Meals: stop about 20 minutes before eating and wait 45 to 60 minutes after. If you need a drink with a meal, make it 7.0.

The handouts disagree on the medication gap: one says 30 minutes before and after, another says 45 minutes after. Use the longer one, and anyone on prescriptions should ask their pharmacist.

Cleaning recipes

From the Nontoxic Spring Cleaning Guide. Mix in glass spray bottles. "EO" means essential oil.

Kitchen

  • Countertop spray: 1 cup 2.5 + 10 drops lemon. Spray, wipe with microfibre.
  • Oven grease: spray 11.5, wait 5 to 10 minutes, wipe with a warm wet cloth. Stubborn spots: 2 tbsp baking soda + 5 drops Thieves, scrub gently.
  • Fridge: 1 cup 6.0 + 5 drops citrus. Spray shelves and drawers, wipe dry.

Bathroom

  • Toilet and sink: ½ cup baking soda in the bowl, then 1 cup 2.5 + 10 drops Thieves. Sit 10 minutes, scrub, flush.
  • Shower and tub scrub: ½ cup baking soda + ¼ cup vinegar + 10 drops tea tree. Sit 5 to 10 minutes, scrub, rinse with warm 11.5.
  • Glass and mirrors: 1 cup 6.0 + 5 drops lemon. Wipe with a dry microfibre.

Bedroom

  • Mattress and pillow mist: 1 cup 2.5 + 10 drops lavender. Mist lightly, air dry.
  • Wood polish: ½ cup 6.0 + 2 tbsp olive oil + 5 drops orange. Wipe on, buff dry.
  • Carpet freshener: 1 cup baking soda + 10 drops Thieves in a shaker. Sprinkle, wait 15 minutes, vacuum.

Living areas

  • High-touch surfaces: 1 cup 2.5 + 5 drops tea tree. Doorknobs, switches, remotes, railings.
  • Couch and upholstery: 1 cup 6.0 + 5 drops lavender. Mist, air dry.
  • Pet beds, toys, litter area: spray plain 11.5, wipe dry.

Laundry and stains

  • Detergent: 4 cups 11.5 + ½ cup Castile soap + 10 drops Thieves. Use ¼ cup per load.
  • Grease: spray 11.5, blot.
  • Wine and juice: dab with 6.0, launder as usual.
  • Sweat: soak in 2.5 for 15 minutes, then wash.

Car and outdoors

  • Windows and mirrors: 6.0 + lemon.
  • Patio furniture: 11.5 + tea tree, wipe clean.

Bottle labels

  • All-Purpose Cleaner: 2.5 + Thieves
  • Glass & Mirror Spray: 6.0 + lemon
  • Upholstery Freshener: 6.0 + lavender

Shopping list: Thieves, lemon, tea tree, eucalyptus and lavender oils, glass spray bottles, baking soda, Castile soap, vinegar, microfibre cloths. The oils in the guide are Young Living.

The story the brochure tells

The trifold walks someone through it in this order. It is a handy spine for a demo conversation.

  1. What's in your water? Tap water carries contaminants. Pitcher and fridge filters mostly take out chlorine and particles. Bottled water carries plastic: roughly a quarter million tiny plastic particles per litre.
  2. Step 1, filter it. Multipure Aquaperform under the sink or in line before the machine. Certified to NSF standards 42, 53, 401 and P231, covers 83+ contaminants including PFAS, heavy metals, pesticides and pharmaceuticals. Solid carbon block, made in the USA. US$695, and Silvia's code 436558 takes 5% off. Replace the filter once a year.
  3. Step 2, activate it. The machine adds dissolved molecular hydrogen gas. The pitch is that hydrogen targets oxidative stress, the wear and tear behind inflammation and low energy.
  4. Try before you buy. The water share lets people drink it daily before they commit to a machine.

The brochure's tap-water lookup (ewg.org/tapwater) only takes US ZIP codes. Here, the equivalent is your city's own water quality report.

Hydrogen talking points (from the intro flyer)

  • Free radicals damage cells over time. That damage is called oxidative stress.
  • Molecular hydrogen is studied as a selective antioxidant, meaning it goes after the most harmful radicals.
  • Research areas named on the flyer: inflammation, brain and cognitive health, heart and blood vessel health, metabolism, energy and mitochondria.
  • For athletes, the flyer cites small studies on less muscle soreness, lower blood lactate, better endurance and power, faster heart rate recovery, and VO2 max.

Water share membership

People pay monthly to pick up fresh hydrogen water before they own a machine. Silvia's version:

  • US$60 a month. Two half-gallon glass jugs, refilled daily with 8.5, 9.0 or 9.5. Up to 1 gallon a day.
  • Billed on the same day each month (she uses PayPal invoices).
  • Members can wipe out the fee by referring someone or attending the monthly water workshop.
  • Her average member buys a machine after about 2.5 months.

The seven steps to set one up

  1. Learn it. Watch the Discovery Process video, then a start-up call with Silvia.
  2. Equipment. K8 or Super501, Aquaperform prefilter, 5-litre BPA-free bags (getjohnelliswater.com), half-gallon glass jars, waterproof jar labels (stickeryou.com). Set aside a clean spot near water and power, with storage and a sanitizing station.
  3. Print. Brochures, the pricing flyer, membership contracts, signage.
  4. Tracking and payment. A member sheet (name, volume, daily pick-up log, notes) and an automatic monthly charge. Onboarding: signed contract, add to sheet, explain pick-up and weekly check-ins, explain the pH ramp-up.
  5. Daily. Fill, log each pick-up, remind people why fresh matters.
  6. Upkeep. E-clean every 1 to 2 weeks, change filters on schedule, spray the spout, caps and jar mouths with 2.5 before refilling.
  7. Grow. Weekly text check-in, ask for testimonials and referrals, invite to the monthly workshop, loaner-unit waiting list, Ukon samples, share the 25-minute web class, track who buys a machine and who joins the business, review quarterly.

The reward amounts don't match across the handouts. Member contract: $50 per referral (after their second paid month) and $50 for attending the workshop. SOP: $40 for each. Referral flyer: $200 for a machine referral, $50 for a filter. 60-day study agreement: $350. Pick your own numbers before you print anything.

The forms in the pack

Hydration Health Assessment (2 pages)
An intake form for someone new. Health history, daily water, exercise, stress, sleep, ten 1 to 10 ratings (energy, digestion, joint pain, focus, skin, sleep, mood and so on), a symptom checklist, and their goals for the water.
Water share member pack
Cover page, a "how to use" sheet (daily ounces, ramp-up dates, meds and meal rules, renewal date, ways to earn the fee back) and a sign-up form (name, phone, email, who referred them, volume, renewal day).
Water Share Log
A blank grid. Name, start or renewal date, then a box for each day 1 to 30 to tick off pick-ups.
Loaner Agreement
For lending a K8 and prefilter. Loan dates, conditions of use, borrower pays for loss or damage, return in the same condition. Page 2 is a tips sheet on the five waters.
60-day Ionized Water Wellness Study (3 documents)
A welcome letter, a signed commitment, and a symptom survey filled in on day 1, day 30 and day 60. The commitment: 6 to 10 glasses a day, no soda, sports drinks or juice, coffee and tea made with the water, normal diet and exercise, weight, blood pressure and pulse recorded, weekly pick-up, $10 fee for an unreturned vessel. The survey covers sleep, energy and mood, digestion, heart and endurance, joints and inflammation, nervous system, eyes and sinuses, hormones, skin, hair and nails, immune, urinary, cravings, and overall wellbeing, mostly rated 0 to 5.

Careful what you repeat

Some of these handouts go further than is safe to say out loud. Silvia's own study agreement puts the line in the right place: the water "is not presented as a cure, treatment, or prevention for any disease."

Fine to say

  • What each water is for and how you use it at home.
  • Your own experience drinking it.
  • "There is published research on molecular hydrogen. Here are the links, have a read."
  • "Try it for a few weeks and see how you feel."

Leave on the page

  • Anything about cancer, including the testimonial with the blood-work chart.
  • "Heals", "prevents disease", "addresses the root cause".
  • "Tap water is no longer fit for human consumption."
  • The named pro athletes. The flyer gives no source for any of them.
  • Income examples as if they were typical.
